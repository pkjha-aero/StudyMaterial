# Books

<span class="status status-working">working</span>

The references cited across this site, gathered in one place. These are the books worth
returning to — not a reading list, but the shelf a page sends you to when its summary runs
out.

## Foundations

| Book | For |
|---|---|
| Trefethen & Bau, *Numerical Linear Algebra* | Conditioning and the SVD, exceptionally clearly |
| LeVeque, *Finite Difference Methods for ODEs and PDEs* | Stability, consistency, convergence |
| Saad, *Iterative Methods for Sparse Linear Systems* | Krylov methods, preconditioning |
| Briggs, Henson & McCormick, *A Multigrid Tutorial* | Short and genuinely tutorial |
| Bender & Orszag, *Advanced Mathematical Methods* | Asymptotics and matched expansions |
| Strang, *Computational Science and Engineering* | The broad survey |
| Wasserman, *All of Statistics* | Compact and rigorous |
| Gelman et al., *Bayesian Data Analysis* | The Bayesian reference |
| Smith, *Uncertainty Quantification* | Forward propagation, sensitivity, calibration |
| Oberkampf & Roy, *Verification and Validation in Scientific Computing* | The V&V standard |
| Roache, *Verification and Validation in Computational Science* | GCI and Richardson |
| Hairer, Lubich & Wanner, *Geometric Numerical Integration* | Symplectic theory |
| Martins & Ning, *Engineering Design Optimization* | Adjoints in a design context |

## Fluids and aerospace

| Book | For |
|---|---|
| Batchelor, *An Introduction to Fluid Dynamics* | Conservation laws, constitutive relations |
| Ferziger, Perić & Street, *Computational Methods for Fluid Dynamics* | SIMPLE, PISO, Rhie–Chow |
| Moukalled, Mangani & Darwish, *The Finite Volume Method in CFD* | Unstructured detail, non-orthogonal correction |
| LeVeque, *Finite Volume Methods for Hyperbolic Problems* | Weak solutions, limiters, TVD |
| Toro, *Riemann Solvers and Numerical Methods* | The Riemann-solver reference |
| Hirsch, *Numerical Computation of Internal and External Flows* | Stability analysis, modified equations |
| Pope, *Turbulent Flows* | The graduate turbulence reference |
| Tennekes & Lumley, *A First Course in Turbulence* | Scales and the cascade, concisely |
| Wilcox, *Turbulence Modeling for CFD* | Model equations and their calibration |
| Anderson, *Fundamentals of Aerodynamics* / *Introduction to Flight* | The standard entry points |
| Katz & Plotkin, *Low-Speed Aerodynamics* | Panel methods |
| Etkin & Reid, *Dynamics of Flight* | Stability and control |
| Mattingly, *Elements of Gas Turbine Propulsion* | Cycle analysis |
| Sutton & Biblarz, *Rocket Propulsion Elements* | Rockets |
| Megson, *Aircraft Structures for Engineering Students* | Thin-walled structures |
| Hodges & Pierce, *Structural Dynamics and Aeroelasticity* | Flutter and divergence |
| Leishman, *Principles of Helicopter Aerodynamics* | Rotorcraft |
| Burton et al., *Wind Energy Handbook* | Turbines and plants |
| Vallado, *Fundamentals of Astrodynamics and Applications* | Orbits |
