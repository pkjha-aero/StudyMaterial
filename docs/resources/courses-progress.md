# Learning roadmap

<span class="status status-working">working</span>

The areas under active development, and how they map onto this site. Organised by the
[Skills Map](../skills-map.md) pillars, so a gap here corresponds to a specific section.

!!! note "This is a roadmap, not a personal progress tracker"
    The planning document this derives from carries per-course completion status. That
    belongs in a private document rather than on a public site, so what follows is the
    *structure* — the areas, what each is for, and where the site covers it. Personal
    status stays where it is.

## Areas with no site section yet

Represented in the planning document but not on the site, either because they are
peripheral to its purpose or not yet developed enough to write about usefully:

- **Reinforcement learning** — would sit alongside [ML](../ml/index.md).
- **Robotics, SLAM, perception, sensor fusion** — a substantial area in its own right;
  would need its own section rather than a page.
- **Agentic AI and LLM application development** — partly covered in
  [LLMs and generative AI](../dl/llm-genai.md), which deliberately stays conceptual.
- **Systems design and engineering leadership** — outside this site's technical scope.
- **Quantum computing** — peripheral.
- **Flight training and aviation practice** — not a computational topic.

Adding a section is a real commitment: the [note template](../how-to-use.md) asks for
failure modes, which means the area has to be known well enough to have failed at it.
