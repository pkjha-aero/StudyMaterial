## What is and is not here

The **course archive is indexed, not hosted**. Roughly 10 GB of coursework lives on
external storage and is never committed; this section publishes enough to locate a folder
— course code, what it covers, extent, and the path — and nothing more. No filenames, no
instructor attribution.

The **references** pages exist because a recap page's *Sources* section is only useful if
you can get from a citation to the thing. Gathering them in one place also makes the
site's dependencies visible: what it is built on, and where it stops.
